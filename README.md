One fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated —
a classic "tall, dark stranger" fortune with Margin Call as the stranger,
rueful and warm instead of cruel.

## The fortune

> You will meet a tall, dark stranger: Margin Call. He emptied your wallet, but left your sense of humour.

19 words, one line, plain text.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (pure stdlib, deterministic —
the same line on every run).
